## 🌟 Features

- **OneNote Notebook Style**: Color-coded side section tabs (*Nuts & Snacks*, *Cookies*, *Candies*, *Fudge*, *Bread*, *Frostings*, *Main Dishes*) matching your physical notebook organization.
- **Complete Holiday Recipe Collection**: Over 17+ classic family recipes with prep/cook times, ingredients, step-by-step directions, handling instructions, and variations.
- **GitHub OTA Updates**: Over-the-air recipe syncing directly from your GitHub repository (`recipes.json`).
- **Mom's Tips & Tricks**: Dedicated holiday baking tips and old-fashioned kitchen secrets.
- **Cozy Holiday Bakery Palette**: Designed with Sugar Dust White (`#FFF9F3`), Gingerbread Brown (`#A35C2A`), Candy Cane Red (`#C23B22`), and Frosting Cream (`#F8E9D9`).
